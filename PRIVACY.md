# Privacy Policy for Saar AI

**Effective date:** November 14, 2026
**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

Saar AI ("the extension", "we", "us") is a Chrome extension that summarizes, explains, quizzes you on, and lets you chat with the web page you are reading. It is built to be private: **all processing happens on your device, and no data ever leaves your browser.**

This policy explains what information the extension handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

---

## Summary

- **We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.**
- **No servers.** The extension has no backend and makes no network requests to us or to any third party.
- **On-device AI.** Summaries, answers, and quizzes are generated by Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano), which runs locally on your computer.
- **No analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies.**
- **Local storage only.** The small amount of data the extension saves stays in your browser and can be deleted at any time.

---

## 1. Information the extension handles

### a. Web page content
When you open the Saar AI side panel, the extension reads the main text content of the **active tab** (using Mozilla's open-source Readability library) so it can be summarized or discussed. This content:

- is processed only on your device by Chrome's built-in AI;
- is never sent to us or to any third-party server;
- is only read when you use the extension, and only from regular `http://` and `https://` pages.

### b. Your questions and generated results
The questions you type, the AI-generated summaries, chat responses, and quiz results are kept in **session storage** (`chrome.storage.session`) so you can switch tabs without losing your work. This data is **automatically cleared when you close the browser**.

### c. Reading history and usage statistics (stored locally)
To show the "time saved" dashboard, the extension stores the following on your device:

| Data | Where it is stored | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| URLs of pages you used Saar AI on | IndexedDB (in the extension) | Avoid double-counting pages and remember per-page stats |
| Word count, estimated reading time, and content category of those pages | IndexedDB | Calculate estimated time saved |
| Seconds spent reading in the extension per page | IndexedDB | Calculate estimated time saved |
| Total pages condensed and total time saved | `chrome.storage.local` | Display lifetime statistics |

The full text of pages is **not** saved permanently. None of this data is transmitted anywhere.

### d. Information we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email address, account details, location, IP address, payment information, health information, authentication data, personal communications, keystrokes, mouse movements, or browsing activity outside of the pages on which you actively use the extension.

---

## 2. How the information is used

Information is used **only** to provide the extension's single purpose: helping you read and understand the current web page (summaries, explanations, Q&A, quizzes, and your personal time-saved statistics). It is never used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or any purpose unrelated to this functionality.

---

## 3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, transfer, or share your data with anyone. Because the data never leaves your device, we have no access to it ourselves.

---

## 4. Chrome built-in AI (Gemini Nano)

Saar AI uses Chrome's built-in AI APIs (Prompt API and Summarizer API). The first time these are used, **Chrome itself** may download the Gemini Nano model from Google. This download is handled entirely by Chrome, not by Saar AI, and is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's Generative AI Prohibited Uses Policy](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy). Once the model is available, all inference runs locally on your device.

---

## 5. Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `sidePanel` | Display the Saar AI interface in Chrome's side panel. |
| `activeTab` | Access the page you are currently viewing when you use the extension. |
| `scripting` | Inject the content extractor that reads the article text from the active page. |
| `tabs` | Detect when you switch tabs or a page finishes loading, so the side panel shows content for the correct page. |
| `storage` | Save your session results and usage statistics locally in your browser. |
| Host permissions (`http://*/*`, `https://*/*`) | Allow text extraction on any website you choose to use Saar AI with. Pages are only read when you open the side panel. |

---

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- **Session data** (summaries, chats, quiz results) is deleted automatically when you close Chrome.
- **Local data** (page statistics and lifetime totals) stays on your device until you delete it.

You can delete all extension data at any time by:
1. **Uninstalling the extension** — Chrome removes all of its stored data, or
2. Going to `chrome://settings/content/all`, searching for the extension, and clearing its data.

---

## 7. Security

Because all data stays in your browser's sandboxed extension storage and nothing is transmitted over the network, there is no remote copy of your data that could be exposed. The extension does not load or execute remote code.

---

## 8. Children's privacy

Saar AI does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

---

## 9. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy compliance

The use of information by Saar AI complies with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the **Limited Use** requirements. Specifically:

- Data is used only to provide or improve the extension's single, user-facing purpose.
- Data is not transferred to third parties.
- Data is not used for personalized advertising.
- Data is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
- No human reads user data.
